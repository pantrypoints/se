Based on the 4 Laws of Value

1

Commonality: Nominal Price

N = (DA,t : DB,t : DC,t) • σ(Di − Dm)

Nominal Price Range

Effective Demand of an individual in the chain

The minimum societally-acceptable demand

Step function where the output is 1 (valid) if Di ≥ Dm, and 0 (invalid/null) if Di < Dm

4

Fair Exchange: Market Price

Mt = k • Pt • γ
Mt
Market Price as the actual value exchanged (markup, fee, or payment) at time t as Exchangeable Value from Law 4
k
The Fair Exchange Coefficient: The agreed-upon "Markup" or "Service Fee" ratio. Unlike interest, k is a percentage of actual output, not a fixed percentage of a past debt. We call this "secondary arbitrage."

Circulation Coefficient (velocity of value transfer), γ > 1 is overcirculation, γ < 1 is undercirculation
